Postura for Business

Ergonomics You Can Finally Measure

Musculoskeletal disorders are one of the most preventable — and most expensive — line items in workplace health. Postura helps employers reduce the onset of MSD claims before they happen, turning a fixed cost into a measurable, manageable one.

Beyond the cushion itself, Postura tracks how well your existing ergonomic investments — chairs, desks, sit-stand setups — are actually being used. Every alert we send and every session we log rolls up into a real-time view of whether employee wellness is trending up, so you're no longer guessing.
